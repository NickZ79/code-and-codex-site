The magic of making your website

Four steps,
start to launch

Most projects run six to ten weeks. You always know which step we are on.

  1. 01

    We talk

    A call or a long email. What the business does, who you need to reach, what the site has to accomplish. No pitch deck.

  2. 02

    A shape

    I come back with a plan, a page map and a fixed quote. You know the number before anything gets built.

  3. 03

    The craft

    Design and build, in the open. You see real pages as they land, not mockups, and you say so when something is wrong.

  4. 04

    Bound and handed over

    Launch, then a walkthrough of how to run it. I stay reachable — most of my work comes from people I launched years ago.

Every Visit

What's actually on the checklist

The exact list your cleaner works from — nothing hidden behind "deep clean" language.

Kitchen

  • Counters & backsplash wiped
  • Sink scrubbed & dried
  • Stovetop & exterior appliances
  • Cabinet fronts wiped
  • Floor swept & mopped

Bathrooms

  • Tub & shower scrubbed
  • Toilet cleaned, inside & out
  • Mirrors & fixtures polished
  • Floor sanitized

Bedrooms

  • Dusting, all surfaces
  • Beds made (linens provided by you)
  • Floors vacuumed or mopped
  • Trash emptied

Living Areas

  • Dusting & surface wipe-down
  • Upholstery vacuumed
  • Floors vacuumed or mopped
  • Glass & mirrors

Whole Home

  • Baseboards & door frames
  • Light switches & handles
  • Trash removed to bin

Add-On Extras

  • Inside oven
  • Inside refrigerator
  • Interior windows
  • Laundry, washed & folded
